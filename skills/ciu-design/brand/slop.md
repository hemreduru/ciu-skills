# Slop Check — what makes a design look generated

Run this list against every preview (SKILL.md §6). A hit is evidence of a default nobody chose: fix it, or be able to say in one sentence why it is intentional.

## Composition
1. Photo + dark bottom gradient + white title bottom-left — the default "AI poster". Use a panel, tint, bar or another skeleton.
2. Everything centered and stacked (logo / title / subtitle / date in one column with equal gaps).
3. Timid scale: the title only ≈ 1.5× the subtitle; nothing clearly dominates.
4. The whole photo shown small and polite — no crop decision.
5. No shared axis: elements float at unrelated positions; or equal margins everywhere with no tension.
6. Filler decoration: random circles, blobs, dots, waves, diagonal stripes, sparkles, confetti, lens flares.
7. Two variants that are the same layout with a color swap.
8. The same skeleton as the previous design in this conversation, with no reason.

## Type
9. Poppins 700 for everything; no weight contrast.
10. A whole headline in ALL CAPS (caps only for kickers of ≤ 4 words — style.md).
11. A random headline word recolored for "emphasis". Allowed only as UKÜ does it: the key word on its own orange bar.
12. A tracked-out caps eyebrow above every block; 01/02/03 numbering when nothing is sequential.
13. One orphan word on the last headline line; ragged, unbalanced line breaks.
14. Text shadow, glow or outline used to rescue legibility on static type — fix the background instead.

## Color
15. Any gradient (purple–blue, orange–pink, mesh, glassmorphism). UKÜ color is flat.
16. Pure `#000` or mid-gray panels; gray text on a colored panel.
17. More than one accent; orange as a large field behind a photo (orange is for bars, numbers and small marks, except campaign covers).
18. A panel color that ignores the photo (a cold navy under a warm sunset photo).

## Brand
19. The logo as the hero, centered on the photo, or on a busy area.
20. Stock-feel icons and clip-art: globes, handshakes, mortarboards, lightbulbs, emoji inside the image.
21. Long sentences, CTAs, URLs or hashtags inside the image — they belong in the caption.

## Motion (video)
22. Everything fades in at once; linear moves; bounce or elastic easing.
23. No exits — elements are simply cut when the scene ends.
24. A frame held for more than 1 s with nothing moving.
25. Every scene uses the same entrance.

## AI tells (added) — what gives a generated design away, and the fix
26. Fake depth: soft drop shadows, glow, glassmorphism cards, 3D chrome. → Flat panel, tint or bar; depth comes from the photo.
27. A stock-looking group photo used as is (everyone smiling at the camera, evenly lit, centered). → Crop to a detail or a face, or duotone/tint it; one subject, one decision.
28. Three equal blocks or an icon + two lines repeated down the canvas. → One hero and one support line; if there are really three facts, make one of them 3× bigger.
29. Hollow copy: "Geleceğe adım at", "Hayallerine ulaş", "Fırsatları keşfet", "Excellence in education" with no fact in it. → Say the fact: a date, a number, a name, a place.
30. Headline built as "Kelime: Alt başlık" or a rhetorical triplet ("Öğren. Keşfet. Başar."). → One concrete statement, ≤ 8 words.
31. Sparkle, rocket, light-bulb and ✨ decoration, or emoji used as bullets inside the image. → Nothing; the hero carries the design.
32. The same corner radius and the same card on every element. → One shape language per design: sharp panels, or a single arch/circle moment.
33. Cheap trend cosplay: Y2K chrome, bubble letters, glitch, film grain, neon glow. → Not UKÜ (flat, honest). Use scale, crop and a sharp color field for energy instead.
34. Low-contrast support text (light gray on photo or on a color field, < 4.5:1). → White or `ink` at full strength; thin weights (300) only at ≥ 3.4 % of canvas height.
35. Tiny type for the info row (date, place) because the title "needs the room". → Info row ≥ 3 % of canvas height; shorten the title instead.
36. Video: every scene zooms in the same direction, every cut is the same fade or push, every line pops on the beat. → Alternate drift direction, vary entrances (wipe / rise / scale), one signature move; hard cuts are fine on the beat.

## Refine pass (after the list)
Do not add anything. Remove one thing and sharpen one thing: alignment, spacing, crop, or the size of the hero. Ask: "What would UKÜ's senior designer delete here?"
